    Tell us about your event

    Working with Cape Fun

    We're a small, owner-run team: the person you brief is the person waiting at arrivals. For larger delegations, we coordinate trusted partner vehicles under one plan and one invoice.

    How many delegates can you handle?

    Up to 14 delegates at the same time (2 vehicles of 7). For bigger groups, we can look after your speakers and VIPs while a coach company moves the main delegation.

    Which currency do we pay in?

    All quotes, contracts and invoices are in South African rand (ZAR). Euro, pound and dollar prices on this page are indicative only, converted at the current exchange rate to help you budget.

    Can the boards show our event branding?

    Yes. Send us your logo and we'll prepare boards with your event branding and each delegate's name.

    What if a flight is delayed?

    We track every flight and adjust the pickup time. 60 minutes of waiting after landing is included.
